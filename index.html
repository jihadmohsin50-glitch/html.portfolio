AI Chess Board:

A visual AI chessboard is a system that uses computer vision to "see" a physical or digital chessboard, interpret the game state, and feed that position into an artificial intelligence chess engine (like Stockfish) for real-time analysis, move visualization, or automated play.

Tech Stack & Hardware NeededBR

Building a prototype requires components across hardware, computer vision, and game logic:
Hardware:

  • Overhead Camera: A USB webcam, Raspberry Pi Camera module, or phone camera positioned directly above the board.

  • Processor: A standard laptop/PC or a single-board computer (like a Raspberry Pi 4/5 or NVIDIA Jetson Nano).

  • Chess Set & Board: High-contrast physical pieces work best for reliable visual tracking.
